AIRLINE PASSENGER POLICY — GENERAL GUIDELINES
(For demonstration purposes. Always verify with the airline.)

==================================================
1. CABIN BAGGAGE
==================================================
- One cabin bag per passenger: maximum 7 kg.
- Dimensions: 55 cm x 35 cm x 25 cm (length x width x height).
- One additional personal article allowed: ladies' handbag, laptop bag, or
  small purse that fits under the seat.
- Cabin bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat in front of you.
- Sharp objects, liquids over 100 ml, and prohibited items are not allowed.
- Liquids must be in containers of 100 ml or less, carried in a transparent
  resealable bag of 1 litre capacity.

==================================================
2. CHECKED BAGGAGE
==================================================
- Domestic economy: 15 kg free checked baggage.
- International economy: 23 kg free checked baggage.
- Single piece must not exceed 32 kg (occupational health regulation).
- Baggage exceeding 32 kg must be split into multiple pieces or sent as cargo.
- Each checked bag must have a name tag with passenger contact details.

==================================================
3. CHECK-IN TIMELINE
==================================================
- Domestic flights: check-in counters open 2 hours before departure and
  close 45 minutes before departure.
- International flights: check-in counters open 3 hours before departure
  and close 60 minutes before departure.
- Web check-in: opens 48 hours before departure and closes 1 hour before.
- Bag drop counter: closes 45 minutes before departure (domestic).
- Boarding gate: closes 20 minutes before departure.
- Passengers arriving after gate closure will not be allowed to board.

==================================================
4. BOARDING GROUP RULES
==================================================
- Boarding is done in groups/zones based on row number or fare class.
- Priority boarding: business class, premium economy, and passengers
  with infants or reduced mobility.
- Group 1: rows at the rear of the aircraft.
- Group 2: middle rows.
- Group 3: front rows.
- Exact group numbers vary by aircraft and are announced at the gate.
- Passengers must have their boarding pass and ID ready at the gate.

==================================================
5. EXCESS BAGGAGE
==================================================
- Excess baggage is charged per kilogram beyond the free allowance.
- Airport counter rate: typically higher.
- Pre-purchased online rate: cheaper if bought before check-in.
- Pre-purchase is available up to 2 hours before departure.
- Excess baggage must be paid at the airline counter or online.
- Carriage is subject to aircraft load limits.

==================================================
6. GENERAL TRAVEL GUIDELINES
==================================================
- Carry a valid government-issued photo ID.
- Report to the boarding gate at least 25 minutes before departure.
- Passengers with special needs should inform the airline 48 hours in advance.
- Electronic devices must be charged and accessible at security.
- Power banks must be carried in cabin baggage only, never in checked bags.
